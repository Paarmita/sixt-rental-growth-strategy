# Sixt Rental Growth Strategy

A Product Management case study exploring personalisation-led growth strategy for SIXT's digital rental experience.

**Live site:** [sixt-product-case.netlify.app](https://sixt-product-case.netlify.app/)

## Overview

This strategy targets a 20% increase in revenue per rental (from €22 to €26.40+) by closing the gap between digital and branch upsell performance. It addresses the problem through three core levers:

- **Moments** — expanding upsell touchpoints across the customer journey
- **Messages** — shifting from product specs to customer-outcome-led messaging
- **Match** — real-time personalisation tailored to business travellers, families, first-timers, and leisure renters

The case study includes a phased 4-quarter roadmap, from foundational UI improvements through to ML-powered personalisation and bundled offers.
