SIXT Mobile Check-in allows you to digitally upload your documents and verify your identity in advance via the SIXT App so you can skip the counter lines and proceed directly to the key pickup. Self-Service refers to fully automated kiosks where you complete the entire rental process on the spot without staff.
Here are the specific differences:
- Mobile Check-in: You upload your driver's license and verify your details 24 to 48 hours before your trip via the app. You then go straight to the Sixt key box or counter, show your ID, and grab your keys.
- Self-Service: You walk up to a physical SIXT digital kiosk (available at select branches). You insert your ID, make your vehicle selection, sign the contract, and get your keys directly from the machine.
- Vehicle Selection: Mobile Check-in often allows you to digitally select a specific vehicle within your reserved category about 30 minutes before pickup. Self-service kiosks typically assign what is available in the system.
