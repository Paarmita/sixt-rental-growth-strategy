Executive Summary

The Gap Between Reach and Revenue

Sixt's digital booking journey already captures the customer — the untapped opportunity is converting them on upgrades, protection, and add-ons before they reach the counter.

Single Compressed Window

The digital journey — from selection to payment — is the highest-leverage moment. Customers are already in a purchasing mindset and open to value-adds.

Products Presented, Not Sold

Current digital flows display ancillaries passively. Framing, sequencing, and personalisation can dramatically lift attach rates across upgrades and protection.

One Size Fits All

No personalisation today. Data signals (past history, loyalty tier, trip purpose) can power contextual, segment-specific offers that convert at 2–3× baseline.

3 Strategic Levers

L1

Earn the Upgrade

Right car · right moment · right price. Dynamic upgrade offers timed to the booking flow — visual, contextual, and segment-aware.

L2

Earn the Protection

Shift from "add on" anxiety to peace-of-mind framing. Tiers, social proof, and post-booking touchpoints to convert the 60% who don't protect today.

L3

Earn the Add-on

GPS, baby seat, extra driver, fuel — prompted by who's renting, where they're going, what they've chosen before. Bundle smartly for higher basket value.

Pillar 1

Earn the Upgrade

Target: +15pp upgrade attach rate → Est. +€8 incremental revenue per rental
1

Dynamic Upgrade Pricing

Price upgrades based on real-time fleet availability and customer segment. Last-minute bookers see steeper discounts; loyal customers get early access to premium tiers.

Show upgrade savings as €/day off RRP — reframe as 'unlock', not 'extra charge'.
2

Visual Vehicle Comparison

Show actual car photos with key feature diff (boot size, passengers, comfort score). Photo-led cards convert 2× higher than spec tables in travel verticals.

Include a 'Most popular for your trip type' badge powered by booking profile.
3

Scarcity & Availability Signals

Display real-time vehicle availability at the selected location. 'Only 3 SUVs remaining' creates genuine urgency — factual, relevant, not manufactured.

Trigger only when availability is genuinely <5 units. Never fabricate scarcity.
4

Segment-Aware Timing

OTS bookers: show upgrade immediately after selection. Business travelers: offer at confirmation with 'one-tap' addition. Families: bundle with add-ons.

Use booking lead time + car class as primary segmentation signal.
Pillar 2

Earn the Protection

Target: +12pp protection attach rate → Est. +€6 incremental revenue per rental
1

Framing Shift — Peace of Mind, Not a Fee

Replace 'Add excess protection for €X' with 'Drive with full peace of mind from €X/day'. Lead with the benefit, not the product name.

Test headline variants per segment: 'International? Stay protected.' for non-EU bookers.
2

3-Tier Protection Architecture

Offer Basic (excess reduction), Standard (+ breakdown cover), Premium (+ personal accident). Middle tier typically drives 50–60% of revenue in tiered models.

Highlight the middle tier with 'Most Popular' badge. Price anchor against Premium.
3

Social Proof & Trust Signals

Show localised proof: '78% of customers on this route add protection.' For first-time renters, add a trust badge and one-sentence claim summary.

Place social proof adjacent to the protection option, not in small print below.
4

Post-Booking Protection Window

Send a personalised email/SMS within 2 hours of booking. Opens a 24-hr window to add protection at booking price — targeting customers who declined at checkout.

~15% expected re-conversion rate on the declined segment.
Pillar 3

Earn the Add-on

Target: +13pp add-on combo attach rate → Est. +€5 incremental revenue per rental
1

Smart Contextual Prompts

Surface add-ons based on booking signals: baby seat for families, GPS for international routes, extra driver if booking name differs from loyalty account.

Show max 2 suggested add-ons per journey — reduce cognitive load.
2

Bundle Offers — Named Packs

'Road Trip Pack' (GPS + Full Tank), 'Family Pack' (Baby Seat + GPS + Extra Driver at −15%), 'Business Pack' (Extra Driver + Priority Return).

Named bundles outperform itemised lists by 30–40% in ancillary travel verticals.
3

Post-Booking Activation Window

After confirmation, open a 24-hr add-on window via email and app. 'Your car is confirmed — personalise your rental.' Lower-friction than checkout add-ons.

Target customers who didn't add any extras at checkout. Expected 8–12% activation rate.
4

Convenience Framing for Business

'Skip the queue — prepay fuel and return empty.' Frame add-ons as time-savers, not cost-adders for business travelers.

Business travelers respond to time value messaging 2× more than price savings.

Experimentation Framework

How We Run Every Experiment

Approach

All experiments run as controlled A/B tests with a 95% statistical significance threshold. Minimum sample size calculated per experiment.


Priority A/B test (high impact, low effort) — 2-week cycles. Strategic tests (higher effort) — 4-week cycles.

Guardrails — Every Experiment
  • MCI completion rate must not fall >2pp below baseline
  • Protection dispute rate monitored weekly
  • Post-rental NPS tracked for every test cohort
10%
Global holdout — no experiments applied — to measure cumulative revenue impact vs. clean baseline
Cadence
Weekly

Experiment revenue impact monitored

Roadmap re-prioritisation based on learnings

Compound Learnings
  • Which segment × ancillary combinations drive highest incremental revenue
  • Optimal placement and timing of each offer in the booking flow
  • Which personalisation signals have the highest predictive power
  • Bundle elasticity — where discounts stimulate vs. cannibalise revenue
